How We Make It

Our traditional process ensures quality and authenticity

Selection

We carefully select the finest quality gram (chana) from trusted farmers.

Cleaning

Thorough cleaning and sorting to ensure only the best grains are used.

Roasting

Traditional slow roasting method to bring out the authentic flavor and aroma.

Grinding

Fine grinding using traditional stone mills to preserve nutritional value.

Quality Assurance

At GOLDEN STAPLES, we follow strict quality control measures at every step. Our sattu is made in hygienic conditions, ensuring it's safe, pure, and nutritious. We never compromise on quality and always deliver the best to our customers.
